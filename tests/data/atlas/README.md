# Atlas samples

Copies of four atlas files from Norns round 7 (github.com/drpaj12/nornsIII, `data/r7/atlas/`,
MIT licence, Copyright (c) 2026 drpaj12): ch_intrinsics stock and h1 (seed 1), diffeq1 stock
seed 1 and h1 seed 4. Format: "Atlas export" in the Norns round-7 plan. The placements come from
VPR runs (VTR, Rose et al., FPGA 2012; VTR 9, Elgammal et al., TRETS 2025; commit 3c9a4d23b2).
The atom and design labels come from the VTR benchmark designs (`diffeq1`, `ch_intrinsics`); these
remain under their upstream VTR licences (see the VTR repository), and only names and placements,
not HDL, are included here.
